# Reviewing a Project Plan

Target: one committed Project plan proposed for human approval, judged against the Project Planner
skill at its governing commit. Fresh eyes are the point: inspect the artifact and cited
ground, not the planning conversation. PASS opens the Project; it does not certify a fictional
complete route.

## Method

1. **Check the shape.** Frontmatter carries `title`, `status`, `area`, `type`, and `linear-project`;
   the six numbered sections remain in order, with First pickable Issues, Later bearings, exact gates,
   and any Not yet specified fog. **Done:** every required element exists or is one finding.
2. **Verify the ground.** Read cited Decisions, paths, patterns, and specifications at the candidate
   commit. **Done:** every material claim matches its source or is one finding.
3. **Test the destination.** Intent, scope, acceptance, and governing design agree; each acceptance
   criterion has observable final proof. **Done:** the human knows what approval fixes.
4. **Inspect the starting route.** Each first Issue is self-contained, vertical, and carries outcome,
   owned paths, blockers, gate, and base branch; hot paths are isolated or serial. **Done:** every
   listed Issue is safe for an Issue Captain to claim.
5. **Respect the horizon.** Later bearings orient without speculative precision. Every in-scope
   outcome is represented by a first Issue, a later bearing, honest fog, or a blocking question Issue.
   **Done:** the map starts the journey without hiding or fabricating what comes later.
6. **Return the review block.** PASS only when the plan can safely reach human approval and open the
   first work. **Done:** every finding names the exact correction.

**Wayfinding Fog is not a gap.** A sharp blocker left after authoritative homework and requiring
human judgment is a `Question` Issue in `Waiting for Human` with `HITL`. Dimmer uncertainty stays
in `## Not yet specified`. A plan that pretends either is settled FAILs; honest placement does not.

**Amendments.** Normal creation, splitting, removal, or resequencing of Issues within the approved
destination is the admiral's expected wayfinding. Review an amendment only when it changes the
destination, scope, acceptance criteria, or governing architecture.

## Checklist

- [ ] Frontmatter and six numbered sections are complete; `dydo check` passes
- [ ] Destination, scope, acceptance, and governing design agree
- [ ] Prior art proves the sources and code patterns actually read
- [ ] Every first Issue carries all five contract fields and is a vertical tracer bullet
- [ ] Gates are copy-pasteable, owned paths isolate parallel work, and first merge order is credible
- [ ] Later bearings stay rough; fog and blocking question Issues are placed honestly
- [ ] Question Issues record homework and block every plan or implementation Issue waiting on them
- [ ] The map includes durable knowledge and integration needed to reach the destination
