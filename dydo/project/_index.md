# Project Knowledge

Durable project knowledge, reviewed delivery contracts, and historical proof live here. Linear is the
only live work graph; repository documents do not mirror its status, priority, assignment, blockers, or
Issue bodies.

## Durable knowledge and proof

- [Changelog](./changelog/_index.md) — completed change and release history.
- [Decisions](./decisions/_index.md) — accepted technical and product choices.
- [FutureFeatures](./future-features/_index.md) — unscheduled repo-native ideas; only a human may promote
  one to Linear.
- [Inquisitions](./inquisitions/_index.md) — multi-lens QA reports.
- [Migrations](./migrations/_index.md) — durable migration, freeze, and assimilation evidence.
- [Pitfalls](./pitfalls/_index.md) — recurring gotchas and constraints.
- [Plans](./plans/_index.md) — reviewed contracts for coordinated, cross-cutting, or
  architecture-sensitive Linear Projects.
- [Releases](./releases/_index.md) — durable release records and Git-tag history.
